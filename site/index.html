How the fees work

  • Buying BMC with ETH: the hook takes an extra 1% of the ETH you pay and sends it, as ETH, to the community wallet . On an exact-input buy it comes off what you send; on an exact-output buy it is added to what you pay.
  • Selling BMC for ETH: the hook takes 1% of the BMC you sell and burns it. The burn permanently reduces total supply; nothing is sent to anyone.
  • On top of that the pool charges Uniswap's 1.25% LP fee on every trade, split 1% to the launcher and 0.25% to IMD.
  • Nobody can change this. The rates and the wallet are immutable constructor arguments. The hook has no owner, no pause, and never blocks a swap. If the PoolManager cannot pay a fee at the moment of a trade, the hook books it as a claim that anyone can redeem with collect().

Utility note: selling permanently removes tokens from supply. Over time this reduces circulating supply, which can support price if demand stays constant or grows. This is not a guaranteed price increase.
